Data policy

Plain language, short version of the long truth: we collect the minimum, and we never sell your data.

Who runs this

No13thfloor is run by Louie Blanche, Miami, Florida. Contact: audit@no13thfloor.org.

What we collect

Anonymous by default. You can get a Floor Score without giving a name or email. In that case we store nothing about you — the score is computed and shown, and the visit leaves no record on our side.

If you choose to give an email (to receive a report), we store:

  • Your name (only if you enter one)
  • Your email address
  • Your cloud provider choice
  • The score snapshot: your inputs and the resulting estimates (grade, oversize ratio, spend at risk, CO₂)

We never collect, see, or store your models, prompts, training data, or cloud credentials. We store only cost and utilization estimates — never your data or content.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data. Not in aggregate, not anonymized, not ever.
  • We share nothing with third parties except the email-delivery service used to send you a report you asked for.
  • No advertising trackers. No tracking cookies; no analytics on you as a person.

Benchmarks

We may publish anonymized, aggregated benchmark statistics (for example, the distribution of grades across all scores) in our public State of AI Waste reports. Benchmarks are always aggregates over many scores — never rows about individual users — and small groups are never reported on their own.

Retention and deletion

We keep records only while they are useful for delivering reports and building anonymized benchmarks. You can ask for deletion of everything we hold about you at any time — email audit@no13thfloor.org from the address you used, and we will delete it within 30 days and confirm.

Security

The site runs on a hardened server with encrypted connections and automated security updates. No system is perfect; if you believe you have found a security issue, tell us at the address above.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will carry a new date here. The short version will not change: minimum collection, anonymous by default, never sold.
